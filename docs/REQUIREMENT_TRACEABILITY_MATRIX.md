## Evidence interpretation

- Passing unit/API tests do not establish browser E2E or PostgreSQL persistence unless that environment was actually exercised.
- Synthetic documents test functionality only; they do not establish real-world classification/OCR accuracy.
- File hashes establish byte equality/mismatch only; they do not establish issuer identity.
- Retrieved regulations are evidence candidates, not approved rules or legal interpretations.
